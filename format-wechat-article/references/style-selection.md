# Style Selection

Choose styles by subject, tone, density, image palette, and reader intent. Recommend three, ranked.

| Theme | Best for | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|
| 简约留白 | essays, interviews, reflective commentary, text-heavy posts | the article needs many comparisons or steps |
| 专业报告 | research, policy, finance, B2B, evidence-led analysis | the desired tone is intimate or playful |
| 知识卡片 | tutorials, explainers, glossaries, checklists, beginner education | continuous narrative is the main experience |
| 清透科技杂志 | AI, software, digital products, future-facing education | the imagery is earthy, handmade, or strongly traditional |
| 温暖生活 | growth, parenting, wellbeing, travel, personal stories | technical authority and dense data are central |

## Ranking Heuristic

Score each candidate from 1–5 on:

- semantic fit: subject and tone;
- reading fit: paragraph length and section density;
- image fit: dominant colors and illustration style;
- component fit: need for definitions, processes, quotes, or warnings;
- audience fit: expected formality and familiarity.

Recommend the three highest totals. Break ties in favor of simpler typography and fewer components.

## Preview Scope

Use the same representative excerpt for every candidate:

- title and lead;
- one major section heading;
- one normal paragraph;
- one image;
- one callout or quote.

Show the palette, heading treatment, body rhythm, image framing, and one special component. Keep the comparison compact so the user is choosing a system, not three separately edited articles.

## Default Choice

If the user delegates the choice, prefer:

1. the theme with the strongest semantic and image fit;
2. `minimal` when confidence is low;
3. `clear-tech` for AI, software, digital media, or futuristic 3D illustrations.
